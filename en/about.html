Who We Are

DESIGN MIIU LIMITADA (also known as “MIIU DESIGN CO., LTD.”) is a web and visual design studio based in Macau, founded on August 19, 2022. We focus on helping small businesses build clear, professional websites and the social media marketing materials that support them — digital assets, printed pieces, and campaign visuals. We also partner with schools to create promotional materials that are easy to understand and visually engaging, so every project communicates the right message to the right audience.

What We Do

We design clear, cohesive visuals across both digital and print. Our work centers on web design (portfolio sites, marketing sites, landing pages), print design (posters, brochures, editorial pieces, magazines), and advertising design (campaign visuals, social media ads, display graphics). Using modern tools like Figma, Photoshop, Illustrator, Blender, and Premiere Pro, we turn ideas into polished, ready-to-use designs—including interactive and AI-related projects where needed.
