Case Study #1

One convention, three brands, three very different booths

For the Asia Super Collectors Convention 2026 we designed three brand booths: eBay, Supernova and Metaverse Cards. Each was planned in 3D – visitor flow, display cases, lighting and brand walls – around the brand’s position and what it runs on the show floor.

The eBay booth is built around a live-stream stage; Supernova uses black and gold to present premium trading cards; Metaverse Cards lets wall-to-wall display cases put the collectibles centre stage.

What we did

A live-stream stage

The eBay booth centres on a live-stream stage, with screens and lighting either side for filming and talking with visitors.

Black and gold

Supernova’s booth is outlined in black, gold and light strips to show off its premium, officially licensed cards.

Cards on show

Metaverse Cards uses large display cases and shelving so visitors can see every collectible up close.
