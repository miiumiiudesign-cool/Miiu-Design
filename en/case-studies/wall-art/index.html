Case Study #4

Turning a plain wall into a welcome for every student

We designed large‑scale outdoor wall art for a primary school, turning plain exterior walls into a colourful, welcoming space that reflects the school’s values.

The visuals are playful and easy for children to understand, while durable materials and careful production ensure the artwork stays bright and intact through daily use and changing weather.

A primary school

What we did

Playful illustration

Hand-drawn children playing, reading and running across a meadow – friendly scenes students recognise themselves in.

School identity

Colours and characters that reflect the school’s values, so the wall becomes a warm first impression for students and visitors.

Built to last

Durable materials and careful production keep the artwork bright and intact through sun and rain.
